MONSTER


Naoki Urasawa’s Monster is a legendary psychological thriller following Dr. Tenma, who saves a boy who grows up to become an apex, manipulative serial killer.

Dr. Kenji Tenma risks his brilliant surgical career to save a young child over a wealthy politician.

That child, Johan Liebert, turns out to be a terrifying, charismatic sociopath.

Years later, Tenma is blamed for a string of gruesome murders committed by Johan.

Ridden with immense guilt, Tenma abandons medicine to track down the monster he revived.

The story masterfully explores whether all human lives truly carry equal moral value.

Set in post-Cold War Germany, the atmospheric setting amplifies the eerie mystery.

Inspector Lunge relentlessly pursues Tenma, mistakenly believing the doctor has a split personality.

Every minor character introduced contributes beautifully to the massive, unfolding web of secrets.

Johan manipulates the darkest flaws of human nature without pulling a single trigger himself.

It stands globally as a slow-burn masterpiece of suspense and philosophical depth.
